About the project

Just ten villas, designed for family life

Viktorya Park Villas is a boutique community of just 10 villas on a 5,200 m² site in Büyükçekmece. The 4+2 and 5+2 villas, from 470 to 590 m², were designed for full family life.

Generous interiors, private living spaces and a carefully planned site: the right balance between privacy and neighbourliness. The project is complete, with delivery and title deeds ready, so owners can move straight in.
