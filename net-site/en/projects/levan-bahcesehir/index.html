The project

Sky and a garden for every home

Levan Bahçeşehir is a low-rise neighbourhood developed by UZM Yapı together with the Başakşehir Municipality. Apartments, semi-detached and detached villas and manors sit alongside a golf course reserved for residents, a tennis court and a full set of shared facilities.

The municipality's institutional experience in real estate meets UZM Group's development experience in one project. For buyers, that partnership is the strongest assurance there is.

Low-rise architecture leaves every home its sky and garden. The golf course is not an outside club; it is for residents only. And so standards hold after handover, site and facility management is entrusted to Emlak Yönetim.

Levan Bahçeşehir is developed by UZM Yapı, UZM Group's construction company, in partnership with the Başakşehir Municipality. basaksehir.bel.tr (opens in a new tab)

What is Levan Bahçeşehir?

Levan Bahçeşehir is a low-rise residential project in Başakşehir, Istanbul, developed by UZM Yapı, UZM Group's construction company, in partnership with the Başakşehir Municipality. It brings homes from two-bedroom apartments to villas and manors together with a golf course, a tennis court, a pool and a spa.

Where is Levan Bahçeşehir and how do you get there?

It is in Bahçeşehir, in the Başakşehir district of Istanbul. With the Sazlıdere Bridge under construction, 5. Levent and İSTOÇ are about a 15-minute drive and Istanbul Airport about 20.

What types of home are there, and how large are they?

Two-, three- and four-bedroom apartments, four-bedroom semi-detached villas, five-bedroom detached villas and six-bedroom manors. Net areas run from 64.83 m² (2+1 A) to 329.89 m² (the detached villa); the manor comes with a 1,159.09 m² garden.

Who can use the golf course?

Residents only. It is not a club that takes outside members but part of the site itself, which is what makes Levan Türkiye's first residential project with its own golf course.

How far along is construction?

Construction is under way and the site is documented at regular intervals in 360° panoramas; the latest tour is dated 25 June 2026. Every dated tour is on this page and on levan.com.tr.

Who will manage the site?

After handover, the site and its facilities are managed by Emlak Yönetim, running the shared spaces and amenities professionally.

How can I find out prices and payment options?

Prices and payment plans are shared individually, by home type. Call 444 70 39, ask through levan.com.tr, or visit the sales office at Bahçeşehir 1. Kısım, 2801. Sk., Başakşehir.
