This notice explains how UZM Group, as data controller, processes personal data collected through this website, in line with Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK").

1.Data we process

When you use the contact form: your name, organisation (optional), email address, phone number (optional), the topic you choose and your message. To keep the form secure: IP address, browser details and the time of submission.

2.Why we process it

To receive your enquiry, pass it to the relevant UZM Group company and reply to you; to assess Levan Bahçeşehir, public-partnership, business-partnership, supplier, careers and press requests; to prevent misuse of the form and keep information secure; and to meet our legal obligations.

3.Legal basis and how we collect it

Your data is collected electronically through the form on this website and processed on the grounds in Article 5(2) KVKK: steps directly related to entering into a contract (c), compliance with our legal obligations (ç), and our legitimate interests, provided your fundamental rights and freedoms are not harmed (f).

4.Who we share it with

So that your enquiry can be answered, your data may be shared with UZM Group companies; with the suppliers that provide our email, hosting and customer-relationship services; and with authorised public bodies on request, in line with Articles 8 and 9 KVKK. Cloudflare Turnstile, used to secure the form, may run on servers outside Türkiye.

5.How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as the purpose requires and for the limitation periods set by law; after that it is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

6.Your rights

Under Article 11 KVKK you may ask whether your data is processed and, if so, for information about it; learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad; ask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected, deleted or destroyed, and for those third parties to be told; object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Send requests by email to info@uzmgroup.com.tr or in writing to Kayabaşı Mah. 15 Temmuz Sk. No: 2, Başakşehir / Istanbul. We will respond free of charge within 30 days at the latest.

7.Sources

  • Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (mevzuat.gov.tr, in Turkish) (opens in a new tab)
  • Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (opens in a new tab)
