About us · since 2016

From land to keys, standing by our word.

UZM Group was founded in Istanbul in 2016 and began developing homes in 2020 with its construction company, UZM Yapı. Today the group has one focus: building, and above all Levan Bahçeşehir, developed with the Başakşehir Municipality.

Our story

Since 2016, the same word.

Our story began in Istanbul in 2016. The guests we hosted were looking for homes they could trust, and that search led us to develop our own projects.

UZM Yapı, the construction company we founded in 2020, completed Viktorya Park Villas in Büyükçekmece and Ağaçelebi Sarayları in Arnavutköy.

Today everything we have goes into Başakşehir: Levan Bahçeşehir, which we are building with the Başakşehir Municipality, defines UZM Group's present and its future.

What we stand for

Vision and mission

01Vision

To be a developer whose word is trusted, one that raises the standard of Istanbul's neighbourhoods.

02Mission

To run every project from land to keys on one principle: a transparent process, sound building and the schedule we promised; and to build neighbourhoods that leave lasting value to their residents and the city.

Method

How we build

Every stage of a project sits with one team, so cost, schedule and quality are managed from one desk.

  1. 01

    Land and feasibility

    We measure what the land allows and whether the project will last, before ground is broken.

  2. 02

    Planning and design

    We shape the architecture and landscape around the zoning and the people who will live there.

  3. 03

    The building site

    We run construction with our own project management and document progress for everyone in dated 360° tours.

    Site diary and 360° tours

  4. 04

    Sales and handover

    We offer the homes at our sales office and on levan.com.tr, and hand over the keys to the standard we promised.

  5. 05

    After handover

    We entrust site and facility management to a professional operator, so standards hold for years.

Milestones

Ten years, briefly

  1. 2016

    A first step in Istanbul

    The group is founded in Istanbul. The relationships it builds with its guests in time open the road to building homes.

  2. 2020

    UZM Yapı and the first villas

    The construction company UZM Yapı is founded; in June, work begins on Viktorya Park, 10 villas in Büyükçekmece.

    Viktorya Park

  3. 2021

    Arnavutköy

    Work starts on Ağaçelebi Sarayları in Bolluca: 13 villas with private pools on a 26,346 m² site.

    Ağaçelebi Sarayları

  4. 2025

    Levan Bahçeşehir

    From December, the building site of Levan Bahçeşehir, developed with the Başakşehir Municipality, is documented in dated 360° tours.

    Levan Bahçeşehir
