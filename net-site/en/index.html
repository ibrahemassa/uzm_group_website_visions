All questions about Levan

What is UZM Group?

UZM Group is an Istanbul-based construction and development group. Through its construction company, UZM Yapı, it has completed villa communities in Büyükçekmece and Arnavutköy since 2020, and it is now building Levan Bahçeşehir, Türkiye's first residential project with its own golf course, in partnership with the Başakşehir Municipality.

Who is developing Levan Bahçeşehir?

Levan Bahçeşehir is being developed by UZM Yapı, UZM Group's construction company, in partnership with the Başakşehir Municipality. The low-rise project, with homes from apartments to manors, is Türkiye's first residential project with its own golf course.

Which projects has UZM Group completed?

UZM Yapı has completed Viktorya Park Villas, 10 villas in Büyükçekmece, and Ağaçelebi Sarayları, 13 villas with private pools in Arnavutköy. Altın Sarayı in Büyükçekmece is also among its first projects. Levan Bahçeşehir is under construction.

Where is the Levan Bahçeşehir sales office?

The sales office is at Bahçeşehir 1. Kısım, 2801. Sk., 34488 Başakşehir, Istanbul. For an appointment or information, call 444 70 39 or write through levan.com.tr.
